# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

json-ty is committed to a welcoming, technically rigorous, and harassment-free
community. Be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

Helpful participation includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- sharing reproducible evidence and acknowledging uncertainty;
- respecting privacy and attribution;
- accepting corrections gracefully;
- keeping disagreements focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption,
or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository activity, issues, pull requests,
discussions, community chat, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [me@jairus.dev](mailto:me@jairus.dev).
Reports will be reviewed in confidence. Project maintainers may edit or remove
content, close threads, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
